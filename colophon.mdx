## How the skin works

<Steps>
  <Step title="docs.json sets the bones">
    Orange primary (`#F58426`), dark by default, Anton for headings and Inter for body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="custom.css paints the warpaint">
    Auto-loaded by Mintlify. Every hero, trading card, scoreboard, and Dolan Watch box is a namespaced `.ki5-*` class.
  </Step>
  <Step title="custom.js drops the confetti">
    A tiny canvas engine — ambient confetti on the homepage, plus a Konami-code burst on any page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
