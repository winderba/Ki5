## The route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Battery Park — the launch">
    Floats loaded at the bottom of the island, the harbor at their backs, confetti cannons armed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Up Broadway — the Canyon">
    North through the Canyon of Heroes, the office towers dumping ticker tape until the street looked like it snowed in June.
  </Step>
  <Step title="City Hall — the ceremony">
    Keys to the City from Mayor Zohran Mamdani. **Alicia Keys performed.** Brunson, Coach Brown, and ownership spoke.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Landmarks across the city — the Empire State Building, One Vanderbilt — had already gone orange and blue the night of the clincher. By parade day, the whole skyline was wearing the jersey.
</Note>
